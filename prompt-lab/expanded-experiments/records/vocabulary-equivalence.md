# Vocabulary experiment: semantic equivalence

Preregistered before either jargon generation. Plain and jargon variants both use one prose paragraph and preserve the same requirement order. The plain prompt is N4, also used for A8. No extra quality tags, objects or references are added. This is an analyst audit of equivalence; terminology can still shift a model's interpretation.

| Requirement | Plain wording | Jargon wording | Equivalence check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas and artifact | portrait 2:3 illustrated atlas poster | portrait 2:3 illustrated atlas plate | Same aspect ratio and illustrated atlas output; plate substitutes for poster. |
| World and tier count | fictional floating ecosystem; five separated tiers; central axis | fictional floating ecosystem; five-tier vertically exploded composition; central axis | Same five-tier stack; exploded describes separation already requested. |
| Spacing | visible air gaps | visible inter-tier negative space | Same unfilled inter-tier regions. |
| Engineering status | imaginative concept art, not validated engineering schematic | imaginative concept art, not engineering-validated schematic | Same explicit fictional status. |
| Tier 1 | circular platform; three slender white cloud-harvesting masts; one curling cloud | circular platform; three filiform white cloud-harvesting masts; one curling cloud | Filiform replaces slender; counts, color and relationships fixed. |
| Tier 2 | exposed filter; different rough basalt, black carbon, pale ceramic layers | exposed filter section; differentiated rough basalt, black carbon, pale ceramic laminations | Same three materials in the same tier; no new porosity or filter mechanism. |
| Tier 3 | one clear glass spherical reservoir, half filled with turquoise water | one optically clear glass spherical reservoir, turquoise water at half volume | Same shape, material, contents and fill fraction. |
| Tier 4 | lush garden, stepped terraces, tiny gold bridges between terraces | verdant garden, stepped terraces, miniature gold bridges connecting terraces | Same garden, stepping, small bridges and topology. |
| Tier 5 | one large copper coil, plume of white vapor | one large copper coil, white vapor plume | Coil deliberately retained; helix avoided because it could impose new geometry. |
| Downward links | four thin copper downward arrows in gaps between adjacent tiers | four thin copper descending arrow glyphs in interstices between same pairs | Same count, hue, weight, placement and direction. |
| Return link | one turquoise arrow outside stack right, curving bottom coil to top cloud | one turquoise return vector exterior right, curvilinear trajectory basal coil upward to uppermost cloud | Same single curved upward link; no extra curvature constraint. |
| Arrow readability | five arrows distinct and readable | visual separation and legibility for five arrows | Same visibility requirement. |
| Title | THE RAIN ENGINE once, large, centered above stack | THE RAIN ENGINE once, large display title, centered above assembly | Exact same string and hierarchy. |
| Tier labels | CLOUD FILTER VAULT GARDEN RETURN, smaller on left aligned to tiers | same five strings, subordinate labels left beside corresponding tiers | Same copy, order, count and placement. |
| Leader lines | simple thin leader lines | simple fine leader rules | Same annotation lines. |
| Allowed text | six strings only; labels readable and clear of illustration | six literal strings exhaust typography; readable annotation scale and clearance | Same exhaustive copy and legibility constraints. |
| Style and palette | elegant museum atlas; fine ink contours; soft3D cutaways; ivory teal moss copper gold | elegant museum-atlas idiom; fine ink contour work; softly modeled3D cutaways; same palette | No new artist, medium, finish, quality token or color. |
| View and light | consistent elevated three-quarter view; light upper left | unified elevated three-quarter perspective; upper-left illumination | No orthographic or isometric requirement added. |
| Detail | intricate mineral, glass, leaf and machined-metal surfaces | intricate mineral, glass, foliar and machined-metal surfaces | Foliar replaces leaf; exact same material families. |
| Margins and exclusions | full stack and labels in generous margins; no people, logos, watermarks or decorative borders | complete assembly and annotations in generous margins; no human figures, logos, watermarks or ornamental borders | Same framing and exclusions. |

The fifteen original language-experiment criteria are reused unchanged. Two jargon outputs will be retained even if they are worse. Model, seed and quality remain unexposed. No result is predicted.
