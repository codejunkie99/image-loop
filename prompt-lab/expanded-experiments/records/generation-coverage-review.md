# Eight ambitious adaptations: generation coverage review

Completed 10 September 2026. Eight built-in generation calls produced eight retained originals. No image was discarded or repaired within this eight-call coverage sample. Prompts, scene requests and 8–12 criteria per image were saved before generation. Every result was visually inspected, printed text manually checked and file dimensions verified.

**Evidence boundary:** these are adaptations of official-guide categories, not matched baseline-versus-method experiments. Subjects, content and sometimes aspect ratios changed. Backend model, seed and quality were not exposed; do not label these verified GPT Image 2.5 runs. Published Sunburst images were separately inspected for qualitative comparison and were not explicitly supplied as generation inputs. No result here proves superiority of a prompt method.

Source images are credited to the [OpenAI prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-prompting?model=gpt-image-2.5). Direct source URLs and local reference copies are recorded. One image per category cannot establish reliability.

## Actionable lessons

- Map depth, action and materials separately: the maritime scene gives every spatial layer a purpose.
- Keep scene boundaries checkable: the dramatic poster delivers copy and scale but its apex touches the headline.
- Simplicity can serve the design: the coastal mark needs a width correction, not more decorative detail.
- Use facts to bound invention: the historical site and topography are sourced; the people and viewpoint remain imagined.
- Map actions as visible orientation and state: returns needs an inward-facing body crossing the threshold.
- Include in-photo signs in the text policy: exact UI labels did not prevent invented photographic slogans.
- Check every leader line: visual polish did not keep the Stoma label on its correct target.
- Keep exact data linked to deterministic output: correct labels do not guarantee mathematically exact chart geometry.

Criterion counts are bookkeeping, not a combined quality score. The small strict chart/export deviations are different in severity from a wrong narrative action or anatomical label. No tolerance was prespecified for exact ratios, so measured approximations are reported rather than silently passed.

## Records and provenance

`generation-coverage-records.json` stores scenes, exact prompts, inputs, source URLs, dimensions and hashes. `generation-coverage-checks.json` stores prewritten criteria, observations and image-specific measurements. PNGs are unmodified copies; the tool originals remain preserved.

`submitted_at` records dispatch intent; `saved_at` records persistence of the returned file, not measured model latency. Inspection was performed by the prompt author without blinding or an independent expert panel. All failures remain available for fresh review.
