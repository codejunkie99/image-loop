## Visible differences independent of condition

- **N4:** Dense miniature garden with two prominent gold bridges; triangular tapered masts; a smooth, broad turquoise return arrow; pale honeycomb ceramic filter. The title is heavy and large. The reservoir has thin framing and an unobstructed central water plane.
- **V6:** Dish-topped masts; pale bead-like ceramic filter; more architectural arches under the platforms; the return arrow becomes flatter and faceted in places. The garden is large and legible. The reservoir has a heavier internal axial mechanism.
- **A8:** Long plain masts with flared bases; jagged floating rock under some platforms; ornamental vertical connectors from the filter toward the reservoir. A stream pours into the glass sphere. More vapor partly conceals the coil center. The right-side arrow is smooth and spacious.
- **S2:** Lighter, more refined title typography; dish-topped masts; clean bead-like lower filter; crisp gold bridges and small waterfalls. The return arrow visually attaches to the copper coil, then climbs along the right edge. Its top and bottom spatial gaps are more deliberate, while its title still sits high.

All four have the intended visual ambition and remain readable. None needs ornate “masterpiece, ultra-detailed, 8K” wording to reach it. This experiment does not support a blanket claim that labeled sections make images more beautiful or more compliant. Its sample is too small to rule out differences; the similar outcomes are simply what these four images show.

## The useful distinction

Both conditions already contain the same ordinary-language decisions: five tiers, specific materials, exact text, countable arrows, position, style and exclusions. The experiment tests **presentation of that information**, not vague versus specific prompting, not long versus short prompts, and not plain versus ornate vocabulary. Headings may still help a person or agent maintain a complex brief and find omissions. The current image results do not establish a rendering advantage for headings themselves.

## If we repaired the weak point

Keep the successful content, arrows and material choices. Replace the ambiguous margin clause with an operational one, such as keeping every title glyph and label inside a 5% safe area and shrinking the overall composition to make that space. That is an untested follow-up hypothesis, not an improvement claimed from these four samples. A true replication would register this change and run fresh matched samples. No fifth image was generated in this trial.

## Provenance limits

The built-in tool exposed no backend/model, seed or quality. The operator supplied no input images or prior-image inclusion arguments. Calls were submitted in alternating condition order, with overlapping execution. Independent sampling here means separate generation calls; unexposed service internals, seeds and session effects prevent a stronger independence claim. These are workflow demonstrations, not verified GPT Image 2.5 measurements.
