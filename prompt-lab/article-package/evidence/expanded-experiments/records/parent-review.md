## One edit versus two

G22 and E22B both achieve the two requested material changes and preserve the major room arrangement. Neither is a pixel-preserving edit. In this one pair, the bundled route achieves the same visible goal in one call; the sequential route needs two and introduces another redraw. There is no evidence here that one-at-a-time is universally better. It remains useful for diagnosis and dependent changes.
