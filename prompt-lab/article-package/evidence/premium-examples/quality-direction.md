# Premium examples: working direction

This collection is for the user's next visual review, before another article rewrite. It consists of original illustrative brand concepts, not client commissions or endorsements. The exact executing image model and quality setting are not exposed by the built-in tool.

## What determines the bar

- Photography: believable light, controlled highlights, visible material texture, purposeful framing, natural anatomy and contact.
- UGC: credible everyday camera language and human texture. Premium does not mean airbrushed or indistinguishable from a studio ad.
- Interfaces: readable copy, coherent selected states, product consistency, practical controls, baseline and spacing discipline. These are raster design concepts, not working apps.
- Graphic design: a clear hierarchy, deliberate type, strong composition, accurate supplied text, a distinctive idea. Do not equate premium with beige or gold.
- Architecture and cross-sections: plausible spatial relationships, straight perspective, accurate relationships between illustrated elements; conceptual drawings are not validated construction documents.
- Every asset: inspect the actual output. Distinguish aesthetic preference from a failed requirement. Keep the original and record repairs honestly.

## First reviewed candidates

- Fashion app v1: strong legibility, consistent bag silhouette, good photo hierarchy. The authored brief incorrectly selected Clothing while showing accessories; the collection screen also retained Discover as active. A focused repair is running.
- Vellum fragrance: clean exact label, distinct paper/glass/metal/wood, coherent reflections and one complete bottle. The cap is taller and the base more visibly veined than the initial direction; acceptable for this concept, not an exact industrial specification.
- Mora UGC: readable product, natural hand contact, visible skin and flyaways, plausible phone framing. It adds a background plant and soap dispenser; no label preservation claim against a real product is made. The mild product sheen is acceptable for the stated morning-routine concept.
