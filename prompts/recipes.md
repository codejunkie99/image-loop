# Twelve original prompt recipes

Status: authored after the two recorded live revision tests; not yet image-tested. Prompts specify visual targets, not guaranteed outputs. Configure actual file dimensions and generation settings through the host when supported, then verify the exported file.

## 1. Quiet technology cover

```text
Create a wide editorial cover about a personal archive of ideas.
Composition: an off-white page fills the frame. On the right, a translucent
smoke-gray cube contains a suspended stack of tiny paper slips. One vivid blue
slip extends out of the cube toward the viewer. On the left, leave calm negative
space and set exactly "A place for unfinished thoughts" on three balanced lines.
Typography: dark graphite humanist sans serif, medium weight, generous leading.
Medium: carefully lit physical studio sculpture with crisp editorial typography.
Lighting: broad soft light from the upper left, subtle contact shadow, visible
paper fibers, no exaggerated glow. Palette: off-white, graphite, one cobalt accent.
Preserve the visual distinction between paper and glass. No interface panels,
floating app icons, extra words, or decorative circuitry. Landscape 5:2 composition.
```

Checks: exact headline; three lines; one blue slip; subject right; copy left; no extra text.

## 2. An everyday object, treated like architecture

```text
Create a square editorial product photograph of a folded brushed-steel desk lamp.
The lamp forms a clear inverted-L silhouette on a pale warm concrete plinth.
Frame the object slightly right of center with its entire base and shade visible.
One narrow amber beam illuminates a small patch of the plinth directly below it.
Use a soft cool ambient fill so the brushed metal grain remains visible; avoid
chrome mirror reflections. The background is a seamless desaturated slate blue.
Style: restrained architectural photography, believable scale, clean geometry.
No text, logos, cables crossing the frame, extra lamps, or colored rim-light effects.
```

Checks: one lamp; full silhouette; amber light lands below shade; steel remains textured; no text.

## 3. A memory workflow people can follow

```text
Create a flat educational diagram on warm white paper, landscape 3:2.
Title: "Turn a thought into something useful".
Arrange exactly four rounded boxes in a horizontal row labeled Capture,
Clarify, Connect, and Create, in that order. One thin black arrow joins each
adjacent pair from left to right. Under Connect, place two small overlapping
paper icons as a visual metaphor, without adding another labeled node.
Add one blue return arrow from Create back to Clarify below the main row,
labeled "Needs another pass" exactly once.
Typography: readable dark sans serif. Use blue only on the return path and
the Clarify box border. No 3D, gradients, fake statistics, or extra stages.
```

Checks: exactly four nodes; three forward arrows; one correctly directed return; exact labels.

## 4. A real-looking repair café poster

```text
Create a portrait poster for a fictional neighborhood repair café.
In the lower two-thirds, show an overhead still life: one cracked red ceramic
mug, a small spool of thread, an opened radio, and a screwdriver on a worn
cream worktable. The objects are separate and easy to recognize, with realistic
wear and no hands or people. Soft afternoon window light enters from the right.
At the top, set "Bring it back to life" in large dark condensed sans-serif type.
Below it, set "Saturday · 10–2 · Community Hall" once, clearly readable.
Use muted red, cream, graphite, and natural wood. Warm, practical, welcoming.
No additional logos, phone numbers, fake sponsors, or decorative tools.
```

Checks: exact copy; four specified objects; no extras; text remains clear at poster-preview size.

## 5. A character designed for continuity

```text
Create a full-body character reference illustration on a plain pale-gray background.
Subject: a small courier robot with a rounded rectangular cream body, exactly two
short legs, two mitten-shaped hands, and a dark horizontal visor showing two
small amber square eyes. A blue canvas satchel crosses from its left shoulder
to its right hip. One tiny triangular notch is visible at the upper-right corner
of its body. Front three-quarter view, feet fully visible, neutral relaxed pose.
Medium: gouache illustration with soft paper texture and clear silhouettes.
Keep proportions compact and friendly. No text, floating props, alternate views,
weapons, extra limbs, or background scenery.
```

Checks: limb count; satchel direction; notch placement; two square eyes; full-body framing. Use this output as an explicit subject reference for later scenes.

## 6. Transparent material, honest edges

```text
Create an isolated product image of a small emerald-green glass perfume bottle
with a clear rectangular cap and no label. Front three-quarter view, entire
object centered with generous transparent padding. The glass should show
thickness, subtle internal refraction, and controlled white studio reflections.
Deliver actual transparency around the bottle, including through appropriate
clear portions of the cap. No solid background, painted checkerboard, pedestal,
text, logos, or drop shadow. Keep the silhouette crisp without a white halo.
```

Checks: decoded transparency; clean silhouette on both light and dark backgrounds; cap intact. Use a transparency-capable tool and format; wording alone does not establish alpha.

## 7. An editorial image about attention

```text
Create a conceptual editorial photograph in portrait orientation.
A single empty wooden chair sits in the center of a shallow pool of still water
inside a bare pale-plaster room. A rectangular patch of morning sunlight falls
on the chair seat and continues onto the water. The horizon is level; use an
eye-height viewpoint with the entire chair and its reflection visible.
Color treatment: warm highlights, neutral shadows, restrained saturation.
Materials should remain tactile: slightly worn wood, matte plaster, clear water.
Quiet and physically plausible. No people, plants, text, surreal extra objects,
fog, lens flares, or dramatic teal-orange grading.
```

Checks: one chair; full reflection; coherent light direction; no extra objects; neutral shadows.

## 8. A small set that belongs together

```text
Create one square image containing exactly three separate editorial spot
illustrations in an evenly spaced horizontal row: a folded map, a closed
notebook with an elastic band, and a small compass. They share the same scale,
slightly elevated viewpoint, thin charcoal outlines, warm cream fill, and one
muted-blue accent each. Keep all three silhouettes entirely visible, separated
by generous empty space on a pure white background. Handmade print texture,
subtle irregularity, no perspective mismatch. No text, letters, numbers,
background shadows, decorative symbols, or frames around the individual items.
```

Checks: exactly three objects; consistent viewpoint and scale; clear separation; no incidental lettering.

## 9. Change typography without rewriting

```text
Edit only the headline in the attached clean image.
Preserve the exact words, punctuation, capitalization, line breaks, left edge,
text box, and color. Make the letterforms narrower and the weight lighter while
keeping them readable. Do not change the photograph, illustration, background,
logos, or canvas. If the requested font treatment cannot fit inside the existing
box while keeping the exact line breaks, identify that conflict before changing
the layout. Do not invent a claim that the original font family was recovered.
```

Checks: exact transcript and breaks; only typography changes; compare protected regions.

## 10. Local color without global grading

```text
In the attached product photograph, change only the fabric panel of the bag
from red to muted forest green. Preserve its weave, seams, folds, highlights,
shadow direction, zipper, straps, logo, and shape. Keep skin tones, background,
white balance, contrast, and all other colors visually unchanged.
Apply a local material-color change, not a green filter over the photograph.
Do not add objects, lettering, or stronger studio effects.
```

Checks: panel green; material detail preserved; no green cast elsewhere; logo and silhouette intact.

## 11. Mix references with explicit roles

```text
Image A is the clean target and supplies all content and layout.
Image B supplies typography characteristics only: its letterform style, weight,
spacing, and hierarchy. Image C supplies local palette roles only.
Apply B's typography treatment to A's existing headline without copying B's
words. Apply C's background and accent colors to the corresponding elements
of A. Keep A's subject, object geometry, arrangement, exact text, and canvas.
Do not import the logos, objects, composition, lighting, or grading from B or C.
If a palette choice makes the headline unreadable, flag the conflict rather than
silently moving it or changing the wording.
```

Checks: roles respected; source content preserved; no imported brand elements; readable contrast.

## 12. Change the atmosphere, preserve the product

```text
Edit the environment around the product in the attached image to feel like a
cool overcast morning. Soften the background light and reduce the warmth of
the surrounding scene. Preserve the product's actual local color, label text,
shape, proportions, position, and camera perspective. Keep the existing props
and their positions. Adjust environmental reflections only as needed for a
plausible scene; do not recolor the product or add rain, fog, droplets, or objects.
Keep the same crop and dimensions.
```

Checks: environment reads cooler; product color and label remain stable; no added weather effects. Escalate if lighting changes make an exact product-color lock impossible with the available tool.
